Proposal: swipe-select for Connections Copilot

Today, grouping a puzzle means dragging tiles one at a time — four drags per group, sixteen per puzzle.

Proposed: long-press a tile and sweep across others to select them, then drag or color them together.

Existing gestures are unchanged: tap still changes color, and a quick drag still moves one tile.

Behavior
0 selected
  • Long-press + sweep — select several tiles (touch or mouse)
  • Drag on empty space — rectangle select (mouse); Shift adds
  • Drag a selected tile — moves the whole selection
  • Tap a selected tile — colors the whole selection
  • Tap empty space or press Esc — clear selection
